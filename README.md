# Hunter Range Bar

In `/hrm`, **Disable targeting section** hides and clears the target list, including its pet, marker and dice buttons, and stops tracking targets. The main bar, Hunter's Mark and cooldown icons above the bar remain enabled. Ability icons below the bar can be disabled separately; their cooldowns are still tracked while hidden. These settings are saved; the targeting section can only be toggled outside combat. The list starts filling again when re-enabled.

A simple addon for WoW Forever that displays the selected target's name and distance in a horizontal bar.

- Without a target, the bar is grey.
- The bar's bronze border, background, fill, marker and font follow the built-in Ranged / Main Hand swing timer, and its initial size matches that timer. The target name is on the left; distance and status are on the right. Out-of-range text is red, and the bar is translucent without a target.
- The green progress bar is empty at 100 yd or more, fills smoothly toward 40 yd, and is full at 40 yd and closer.
- When the client provides exact distance (`UnitDistanceSquared` or target `UnitPosition`), the fill and yardage use it, and the text shows the remaining distance to 40 yd.
- Enemy distance is usually unavailable. The addon checks the 100 yd and 45 yd boundaries using `C_Item.IsItemInRange`, and 40 yd using Arcane Shot. Between boundaries it **estimates** distance from player movement since the last crossing, using position or movement speed. Estimates are marked `~`, work best when moving directly toward a stationary target, and stay within the verified range band.
- Beyond 100 yd the bar is empty and reads `>100 yd`. When Arcane Shot confirms range, it fills and reads `<=40 yd | IN RANGE`. On entering range the bar flashes twice and keeps a bright green border while the target remains in range.
- The **dead zone** (`<8 yd | DEAD ZONE`, where neither ranged nor melee attacks work) has a red fill and border; **melee** (`<=5 yd | MELEE RANGE`) is orange. Zone changes flash in the new zone's colour. Melee is checked with 5 yd items or Wing Clip / Raptor Strike and only considered when Arcane Shot is out of range. The dead zone uses 10/20 yd items; without client support it can only be detected when moving away from melee, not when approaching from ranged distance.
- Hunter's Mark appears as an icon and countdown left of the bar, with a thin red outline around the bar. The addon displays readable marks from any hunter. When you cast it, the addon clears previous cached marks and assumes it landed on your currently selected target; an estimated two-minute countdown is marked `~` until the actual expiry is readable. In combat, known marks are retained until their known expiry even if the aura lookup returns no mark, because that can mean the aura is hidden. Resists, early removal, other hunters' marks, or macros targeting a different unit can make the display inaccurate. Hidden auras cannot reveal early removal or refresh. Returning to a cached target during combat requires a non-secret GUID; targets are never matched by name. Cached states are cleared after combat and on entering the world.
- **Active aspect:** your Hawk, Monkey, Cheetah, Pack, Beast or Wild aspect appears as an icon to the right of the bar. It follows your own aura when readable and otherwise uses your last cast; hidden aura data can leave the display stale until the aura becomes readable again.
- Friendly targets that cannot be checked with ranged abilities still use an approximate distance estimate.
- **Cooldown icons above the bar:** an icon appears on the right after a spell is used and travels left during its cooldown. It has a shadow, a bronze border (red for stings), a clock-like sweep, and a countdown that turns yellow below 5 seconds and orange-red below 2. At the left edge it flashes twice over 0.6 seconds (green for spells, red for stings) and disappears. Overlapping newer icons move up by about four-fifths of their height and retain their row instead of jumping back down. Auto Shot, spells with only a global cooldown, and cooldowns longer than 5 minutes are not displayed above or below the bar or announced; exactly 5 minutes is included. Serpent, Scorpid and Viper Sting instead track their target debuff duration, using the aura when available or 15/20/8 seconds as fallback. If a previously readable aura disappears, its countdown ends early. On target change, loss or detected death, the debuff icon is removed without a final flash or announcement. Cooldown resets and reductions update the icon.
- **Ability icons below the bar:** used offensive abilities and DoTs appear in a horizontal row from left to right. Serpent, Scorpid and Viper Sting track their target debuff duration. During a cooldown or tracked DoT the icon is grey and shows a countdown; when ready it flashes green and remains green until the ability is used again. Right-click an icon to remove it until the ability is used again. `/hrb clear` or `/reload` clears the row. Defensive and support abilities are excluded. The cooldown icons above the bar travel independently.
- **Settings:** `/hrm`, `/hrb` or `/hrb config` opens settings, also available under Esc → Options → AddOns → Hunter Range Bar. **Lock position** disables dragging. **Disable ability icons below the bar** hides the row while continuing to track cooldowns. Set bar width (100–1000), height (10–80) and X/Y position from screen centre using sliders, the mouse wheel or by typing a number and pressing Enter. The defaults button restores the swing timer size and default position. Settings are saved.
- **Completion announcements:** when a travelling icon disappears, its name appears at the configured position, yellow for spells and red for debuffs. An available icon appears to the left, with its detected keybinding in the top-right corner. For a DoT this is the binding of the spell that applied it; no label is shown if no binding is found. Icon and label scale with font size. The announcement flashes twice at full opacity for 0.6 seconds, then the text, icon and binding fall and fade together over the configured duration. Target change or loss clears its debuff icons and announcements; death clears them when the client exposes death or zero health. In `/hrm`, disable announcements or adjust X from screen centre, Y down from the top, font size (16–72), and fade duration (0.5–10 seconds). Defaults: enabled, X = -438, Y = 427, size 38, fade 3.5 seconds.
- **Minimap button:** left-click the Hunter Range Bar icon to open or close settings. Drag it around the minimap to reposition it; its position is saved.
- **Icon size:** `/hrm` sets cooldown icons above and ability icons below the bar separately (24–80, default 39). Changes apply immediately and are saved; the defaults button resets both sizes.
- **Opacity:** outside combat, set a separate value (0–100%) for a living attackable target. Until changed, the normal value is used. Without a target the bar is additionally faded. In combat opacity is always 100%.
- **Language:** choose `Cestina` or `English` in settings. This saved choice changes settings, bar text, tooltips and addon messages. Czech chat text omits diacritics for font compatibility.
- **Target list:** outside combat, selected enemies are added to a vertical list left of the bar, with a separate row even for matching names. New rows appear above the first, which aligns with the bottom of the bar. Each shows health, name, percentage, raid marker and range (`40`, `45`, `100`, `>100` or `?`); a coloured stripe indicates range and a gold border marks the current target. Targets beyond 100 yd remain listed. Death can only be checked for units exposed by the client. Left-click a row to target using secure `/targetexact name`; matching names may select another enemy. Right-click anywhere on a row, including the pet and marker buttons, to close it. It flashes red for 0.5 seconds and is removed outside combat, or remains red until combat ends. New rows are added only outside combat and click actions cannot be reconfigured during combat. The pet button sends your pet to the specific unit using an available nameplate or focus token without changing your target; enable enemy nameplates (V) for actions on specific enemies. Marker button: left-click for skull, Ctrl+left-click for the next available marker, Shift+left-click to remove it. Markers use the secure `raidtarget` action. The dice assign random markers to up to eight rows, including in combat, without changing your target. `/hrb seznam` clears the list; in combat rows are removed after combat. Health is displayed even when returned as secret values.
- Drag the bar with the left mouse button. `/hrb lock` toggles locking, `/hrb unlock` unlocks it, and `/hrb reset` restores the default position.
- `/hrb debug` prints measurement availability, `UnitPosition` and the 40/45/100 yd range checks to chat.

## Installation

Addon destination folder:

`D:\Games\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\HunterRangeBar`

`Lato-Regular.ttf` is needed to display Czech diacritics in the settings, bar and button tooltips. Lato is distributed under the SIL Open Font License in `Lato-OFL.txt`.

Enable the addon on the character selection screen. If the client marks it as out of date, enable loading out-of-date addons.

For enemies, the distance estimate uses only Auto Shot (75), Concussive Shot (5116) and Arcane Shot (3044); interaction and melee checks are ignored because they may return conflicting results for hostile targets. Interaction checks are used for friendly targets. Estimates are marked `~`. Exact enemy yardage is shown only when the client actually provides it.
